Why give

Why your donation matters

Conservation depends on ordinary people choosing to act. No gift is too small: whether you give once or regularly, every contribution brings the creek closer to a greener, healthier future.

  • Plant and nurture

    Your gift raises mangrove seedlings, plants them and cares for them until they can thrive on their own.

  • Restore habitats

    It brings damaged stretches of the creek back to life for the fish, crabs and birds that depend on them.

  • Support the community

    When local people have training, tools and fair income from protecting nature, conservation lasts for generations.

Donate equipment

Prefer to give something practical? Buy any of the items below and send or deliver them to us. Every item goes straight into the field.

  • Crab nests

  • Beehives

  • Beekeeping gear

    Protective suits, gloves, smokers and honey harvesting tools.

  • Boat machinery and equipment

    Outboard engines, spare parts, oars and anchors for our creek boats.

  • Safety gear

    Life jackets and gumboots for planting and boat trips.

  • Nursery supplies

    Potting bags, shade nets and watering cans for raising seedlings.

  • Planting tools

    Spades, machetes, rakes and wheelbarrows.

  • Monitoring equipment

    Binoculars, GPS units and measuring tapes for research and bird watching.
