Work with us for the long term

We partner with organizations that share our goal of healthy mangroves and a thriving community. Our partners bring skills, people and resources; we bring restoration sites, a working nursery, local knowledge and a committed community team.

Areas we partner on

  • Restoration and conservation

    Joint projects to replant degraded areas and protect existing mangrove forest.

  • Research and monitoring

    Studies on mangrove growth, survival, carbon and biodiversity, using our sites and data.

  • Community livelihoods

    Supporting beekeeping, crab farming and eco-tourism that give the community income from protecting nature.

  • Education and awareness

    Programmes with schools and the wider community on why mangroves matter.

  • Technical and in-kind support

    Training, expertise, equipment and services that strengthen our work.

Who we work with

  • Universities and research institutions
  • Non-governmental and community-based organizations
  • National and county government agencies
  • Companies and businesses
  • International and development organizations
  • Schools and colleges

How a partnership works

  1. Reach out. Tell us about your organization and what you would like to achieve.
  2. Meet and plan. We meet, visit the creek together and agree on shared goals.
  3. Formalise. We sign a memorandum of understanding (MoU) setting out each side's role.
  4. Work together. We carry out the work and share regular progress reports.

Programme 1

Adopt a site

A site is a piece of land bought specifically for mangrove planting, measured in hectares (ha). When you adopt a site, your sponsorship funds that land and its planting, so a whole area of new mangrove forest grows under your name.

What you receive

  • A site in your name, marked with a sign at the site.
  • Planting days on your site with your team and ours.
  • Regular progress reports with photos and survival figures.
  • Recognition as a sponsor on our website.

Sites can be adopted from part of a hectare upwards. Contact us for current sites and prices.

KES200 per tree

Programme 2

1 Million Tree Campaign

Help us plant one million mangroves. You pay for the mangrove trees themselves, and you can join our team to plant them. The campaign takes each tree all the way from our production nursery to planting in the creek.

348,250 trees planted Goal: 1,000,000

The journey of your trees

  1. 1

    Collection

    Our team collects healthy mangrove propagules from the forest.

  2. 2

    Production nursery

    Seedlings are raised and cared for in our production nursery.

  3. 3

    Planting day

    Your team joins ours to plant the seedlings in the creek.

  4. 4

    Growth updates

    We monitor the young trees and share their progress with you.
