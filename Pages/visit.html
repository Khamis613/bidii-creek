On the water

Boat rides through the mangroves

Our guides take you deep into the creek, where the mangrove roots rise from the water and the forest comes alive with birds, crabs and fish. It is the best way to see and understand the mangroves up close.

Choose a quiet, traditional canoe or a faster machine boat. Whichever you pick, the price covers the whole experience:

  • Bird viewing: spot herons, kingfishers and other birds that feed and nest in the mangroves.
  • Mangrove restoration: plant a mangrove seedling yourself and help restore the creek.
  • Sightseeing: enjoy the views of the creek and learn its story from our guides.
