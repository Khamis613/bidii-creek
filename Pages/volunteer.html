Schools and institutions

Learn by doing at the creek

Schools, colleges, universities and other institutions can volunteer in our events. It is a hands-on way for students to learn about mangroves, the creek and why conservation matters.

What your group can do

  • Plant mangrove seedlings during our planting events.
  • Take part in creek clean-ups.
  • Learn about mangroves and the wildlife they shelter from our team.
  • Start or support an environmental club at your school.

Groups are welcome with their teachers or group leaders, who stay with their students throughout the event.

Organizations

Plant with us as a team

Companies, NGOs, community groups and other organizations can volunteer by becoming part-time members during our tree planting events. Your team joins us on planting days and works alongside our community.

What to expect

  • Plant mangroves alongside the Bidii team on scheduled planting days.
  • A meaningful team activity that gives back to the environment.
  • See the impact of your work as the mangroves you planted grow.

Individuals

Join the Bidii CBO team

Want to volunteer on your own? Join the Bidii community-based organization (CBO) team and take part in planting and the other activities planned during our events.

You can help with

  • Mangrove planting on event days.
  • Raising seedlings at our nursery.
  • Creek clean-ups.
  • Community education and other planned event activities.

Students on Industrial attachment

Students in related fields can come for attachment and gain real field experience in mangrove conservation, restoration, research and community work.

Related fields of study

We accept students taking courses in:

  • Environmental Science
  • Marine Science and Biology
  • Forestry
  • Natural Resource Management
  • Wildlife Management
  • Fisheries and Aquaculture
  • Botany and Zoology
  • Climate Change Studies
  • Geography and GIS
  • Agriculture and Beekeeping
  • Community Development
  • Tourism and Hospitality

Requirements

  • Currently enrolled at a recognised university, college or TVET institution.
  • Taking a course in one of the related fields listed.
  • An attachment or introduction letter from your institution.
  • An up-to-date CV.
  • A copy of your student ID.
  • Personal accident or medical insurance cover.

Attachment periods are agreed with each student. Please apply early, as places are limited.
