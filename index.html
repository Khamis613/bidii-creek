Who we are

A community standing up for its mangroves

BIDII CREEK CBO is a community-led conservation group dedicated to the restoration, protection, and sustainable management of mangrove ecosystems along Kenya’s coast. Over 10 years of active field experience in mangrove restoration, the conservancy has restored over 5.4 million mangrove trees across multiple sites alongside Tudor Creek in Mombasa County through community mobilization and partnerships. Based in Jomvu, Mombasa, the organization brings together local residents to restore degraded mangrove areas while promoting alternative livelihoods that reduce pressure on marine and coastal resources.

Our Mission: To protect, restore, and sustainably utilize mangrove ecosystems while empowering local communities through nature-based solutions and livelihood opportunities.

Our Vision: To achieve a resilient coastal ecosystem where communities and nature thrive together through sustainable conservation and livelihood development.

Our work

Featured projects

A look at the work currently under way along the creek.

Restoration

Creek Shoreline Replanting

Replanting cleared and degraded stretches of the creek with site-matched mangrove species, then monitoring each plot until the young trees are established.

Biodiversity

Creek Wildlife Protection

Safeguarding the birds, crabs and fish that breed among the mangrove roots, and patrolling against illegal cutting.
